Every soft target makes the same trade. The top-left cell rises and the happy diagonal falls: rl_raw takes neutral recall 67.4 → 71.7 while happy drops 61.3 → 59.8, and rl_lowrank does the same at 70.5 and 58.6. The gain comes from neutral, not from everywhere.

The two α arms go the other way. rl_dir and rl_dir_hi drop neutral (65.1 and 61.1) and raise sad (69.5 and 70.3). Same direction as the affect-geometry mixup target, and both lost.

SAMSEMO, change from the hard-label baseline

What each target moved

Sad is where SAMSEMO pays. rl_raw sends 32.3% of sad utterances to neutral against the baseline's 27.6%, and sad recall falls 62.9 → 58.8. rl_lowrank is milder at 31.5 and 60.2. That is the cost of the neutral gain, and it is larger here than on IEMOCAP.

It also matches the substitution kernel measured on the training corpus, where an annotator who said sad was likelier to be answered with neutral (0.425) than with sad (0.385). Sad is the least self-consistent class in the data, and the models inherit that.

One run per arm. On this split every seed produces bitwise identical logits, so these are exact numbers with no variance estimate attached. rl_kernel was still training when this was built.

Raw argmax on saved logits, no calibration. Branch levers, arms in configs/class_weight.yaml, predictions under checkpoints/<arm>_seed42/predictions/. Trained on 58,997 official-Train rows for 8 epochs; neither evaluation corpus is seen during training.
